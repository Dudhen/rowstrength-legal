Privacy Policy for RowStrength

Effective date: September 27, 2026

RowStrength is developed and operated by Arsenii Dudchenko (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information RowStrength processes, why it is processed, how it is stored, which service providers may receive it, and what choices and rights users have.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact: arseny.dudchenko1998@gmail.com.

1. Information We Process

Account information

If you create or sign in to a RowStrength account, we process your email address and an internal user identifier associated with your account. Your email address is used to authenticate you through a one-time verification code and to maintain your RowStrength account. Authentication is provided through Supabase.

Profile photo

You may optionally select a profile photo. The selected image may be stored locally on your device for display and caching purposes, uploaded to private Supabase Storage, and associated with your RowStrength account through its storage path. Profile photos stored remotely are not intentionally made publicly accessible. Access is restricted through authentication and storage access policies. You may remove or replace your profile photo from within RowStrength.

Free calculation usage

RowStrength keeps track of the number of free calculations used during the applicable monthly period. For signed-in users, your RowStrength user identifier, the relevant usage month, and the number of free calculations used may be stored in Supabase. This information is used only to enforce and synchronize the free-use limit across sessions and devices.

Subscription and purchase information

RowStrength offers subscriptions through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google. RowStrength does not receive or store your full payment card information. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and Premium access. RevenueCat may process your RowStrength user identifier used as the RevenueCat App User ID, subscription and purchase history, transaction and receipt information, Apple receipt information or Google purchase tokens, basic technical information relating to the device and operating system, and information required to determine whether Premium access is active. This information is used to provide subscription functionality, validate purchases, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and determine access to Premium features.

Calculation inputs

RowStrength allows you to enter information such as sex, body weight, rowing distance and time, barbell exercise, weight lifted, and number of repetitions. These values are used to perform RowStrength calculations. In the current version of RowStrength, these calculation inputs and calculated performance results are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Information stored locally

RowStrength may also store information locally on your device, including the selected language, selected section of the application, free calculation usage information, a local cached copy of your profile photo, and other application state necessary for normal operation.

2. How We Use Information

We process information as necessary to create and authenticate RowStrength accounts; provide and maintain the application; synchronize free calculation usage; provide Premium subscriptions; verify and restore purchases; display and manage optional profile photos; prevent unauthorized access or abuse; respond to user support and privacy requests; and comply with applicable legal obligations. RowStrength does not currently use personal information for third-party advertising and does not currently include advertising SDKs or behavioral advertising functionality.

3. Service Providers

Supabase

RowStrength uses a self-hosted Supabase deployment for user authentication, database services, private file storage, and server-side functions used for account management. This primary RowStrength backend is hosted on infrastructure located in Russia. Supabase software is used as part of this self-hosted backend, while technical and network information may also be processed as necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the service.

RevenueCat

RevenueCat provides subscription-management infrastructure. RevenueCat receives information required to associate subscription status and transactions with RowStrength users and to provide Premium entitlement functionality.

Apple and Google

If you purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed by the relevant application store: Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process payment and transaction information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to the service providers described in this Privacy Policy when necessary to operate RowStrength; when required by applicable law, court order, or lawful governmental request; or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of RowStrength, its users, or others.

5. Data Retention

Account information, usage records, and profile information may be retained while your RowStrength account remains active. A profile photo may be retained until you replace it, delete it, or delete your account. Local application data remains on your device until it is removed by RowStrength, you delete the relevant information, clear application data, or uninstall the application, depending on the type of data. When you delete your RowStrength account through the application, RowStrength is designed to delete the account and associated RowStrength data stored in Supabase, including profile files and free-usage records associated with that account. Some transaction and subscription information may continue to be retained by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or other service providers as required for transaction records, fraud prevention, legal compliance, accounting, or operation of their services. Such information is subject to the applicable provider's retention practices.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You may delete your RowStrength account directly from within the application. Deleting your RowStrength account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Subscriptions must be cancelled through the subscription settings of the relevant store. After account deletion, RowStrength removes the account and associated RowStrength data under our control as described above. You may also contact arseny.dudchenko1998@gmail.com for privacy or deletion-related questions.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through RowStrength. These measures include authentication, access-control policies, private storage for profile photos, and server-side account deletion mechanisms. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. International Processing

RowStrength's primary self-hosted Supabase backend is hosted on infrastructure located in Russia. Other service providers used by RowStrength, including RevenueCat, Apple, and Google, may process information in countries other than Russia or the country in which you reside. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the relevant provider's terms, privacy practices, and applicable legal or contractual safeguards.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent where applicable, and the right to lodge a complaint with an applicable data protection authority. You may exercise applicable rights by contacting arseny.dudchenko1998@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

10. Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy applies to RowStrength's own processing of information. Third-party services used by RowStrength, including Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google, maintain their own privacy policies governing their respective processing activities.
